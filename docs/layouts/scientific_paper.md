# scientific_paper

Academic paper page: structured headings, abstract, figures, equations, references. Primary source
of `formula` and numbered `heading`/`subheading` structure.

**Page geometry**
- Width ~1240px; margins 70–120px; 1 column (60%) or 2 columns (40%).
- Body 14–18px; tighter line-height (1.4–1.6).

**Block sequence**
1. `title` — paper title (centered, may wrap 2 lines).
2. `caption` — authors + affiliation line.
3. `heading` "អរូបី / Abstract" + a `text` abstract paragraph.
4. Numbered `heading` ("១. …") each with `subheading` ("១.១ …") and `text` paragraphs.
5. `formula` blocks on their own line (numbered right-aligned sometimes).
6. 1–2 real `image`/`charts` figures with `caption` ("រូបទី …").
7. 0–1 small `table_region` with `table_head`+`table_cell`.
8. `heading` "ឯកសារយោង / References" + `list_item` reference entries.

**Real images used:** `charts`, `photos`, `drawings` (diagrams).

**Diversity knobs:** 1/2 column, equation count, figure/table presence, numbered vs unnumbered
sections, reference count.

**Sampling weight:** 1.5
